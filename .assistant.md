## 2026-05-21 - Reyna/Razka calibration failure diagnosis and governance fix

- Root cause of the bad-looking latest run:
  - Reyna was not failing because the simulation formula was broken or because rollback happened.
  - The optimizer objective and the reported validation RMSE were misaligned, and the current parameter registry over-constrained part of the old good basin.
  - Important bound mismatch found: BSA-scaled/reference values such as `C.PAR` and some `V0.*` seeds could sit outside the active feasible range, so the optimizer could not recover known-good physiology.
  - Sparse catheter cases such as Razka were being penalized by LVEDP/RVEDP even though no LV/RV volume anchors exist to identify passive chamber stiffness/preload from those pressures alone.
- Code changes made:
  - Added scaled-reference-in-bounds protection in `config/build_parameter_registry.m`.
  - Relaxed selected physiologic bounds for `V0.*`, atrial elastance, and RV elastance ceiling so strong candidates are warnings, not automatic hard failures.
  - Added Stage F validation-facing polish in `src/calibration/run_validation_gate_polish.m` and wired it from `src/calibration/run_calibration.m`.
  - Added `validation_holdout` target tier in `src/calibration/build_target_tiers.m`; sparse `LVEDP`/`RVEDP` are visible in full transparent validation but excluded from calibration and governed primary RMSE when no chamber volume anchors are available.
  - Updated calibration weighting/objective/gate polish to exclude `validation_holdout` like `derived_validation`.
